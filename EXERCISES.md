## How to Use This Index

1. **Pick an exercise** from the list above
2. **Read the problem** (problem.md)
3. **Try to solve it** using the starter template
4. **Compare with solution** to see alternative approaches
5. **Do the bonus challenges** to go deeper

## Difficulty Scale

- ⭐ Easy: Can solve in 15-20 minutes with lesson knowledge
- ⭐⭐ Moderate: Requires combining concepts from lesson
- ⭐⭐⭐ Hard: Requires research or creative problem-solving

## Tips for Working on Exercises

1. **Read the problem carefully** - Understand what's being asked
2. **Don't jump to the solution** - Struggle is where learning happens
3. **Test edge cases** - Think about boundary conditions
4. **Write comments** - Explain your reasoning
5. **Compare approaches** - See how the solution handles it
6. **Do the bonus challenges** - That's where mastery comes from
